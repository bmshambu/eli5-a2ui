## Recurring patterns

1. **Full-bleed blue cover**: Cobalt `#1E49E2` → KPMG Blue gradient background, white KPMG logo top-left, very large KPMG Bold title bottom-left in two colours, cut-out or duotone photo on the right. (tech report p1, thought leadership p1, annual report p1)
2. **Photo cover, blue overlay**: full-bleed photo with a blue colour wash, white headline over the top (annual report p1, "Management's review" divider).
3. **Contents page**: dark blue background, big white KPMG Bold page numbers in a 3 × 3 grid with short labels (tech report p2); or a white page with a photo mosaic (annual report p2).
4. **Editorial text page**: white background, KPMG Bold headline in KPMG Blue top-left, body in 2–3 columns, all text KPMG Blue, grey quote/bio panel (tech report "Foreword").
5. **Card grid / newsletter**: white page, square blue bullet + KPMG Bold section label, 2 × 2 cards each with a 1100 × 443 blue duotone banner photo, title, blurb and "Read more"/QR links (thought leadership p2–p5).
6. **Contacts page**: headshots (square-ish, light grey/office background) with name in KPMG Blue bold, title in grey (tech report last page, annual report p31–p32).
7. **Back cover**: solid Cobalt/Purple gradient, white logo, address block, social icons, QR code, legal text (thought leadership last page).
8. **Infographic / KPI page**: light blue tint panels (`#E9EDFC`/`#D2DBF9`) with large numbers in Cobalt, Pacific Blue and Purple accents (Impact Plan).

## Common rules
- Logo always top-left, white on blue or KPMG Blue on white, with clear space at least the height of the "K".
- Generous margins (about 4–5% of page width) and left-aligned text everywhere.
- Strap line "KPMG. Make the Difference." appears bottom-left on covers.
- Footer: small legal/copyright line bottom-left, page number and publication name bottom-right.
