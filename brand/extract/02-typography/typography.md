# KPMG typography (observed in 2026 PDFs)

Measured from the text layer of all five source PDFs (font name, point size, character count).

| Role | Typeface seen in PDFs | Office-safe fallback | Observed sizes | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / headline | **KPMG Bold** (condensed brand display face) | Arial Narrow Bold / Arial Bold | 100, 60, 44, 42 pt on covers; 27–18 pt section titles | KPMG Blue `#00338D` on white, White on blue |
| Sub-heads | Univers LT Std Bold | Arial Bold | 26, 24, 21, 17, 14 pt | KPMG Blue / Cobalt `#1E49E2` |
| Body (long-form report) | Univers LT Std Light / Univers 45 Light | Arial | 17 pt (1920 px slide), 12–14 pt (A4/16:9) | KPMG Blue `#00338D` or Grey 60 `#666666` |
| Body (annual report, Office decks) | Arial (ArialMT) | Arial | 9 pt body, 7 pt notes, 5–6 pt legal | KPMG Blue `#00338D` |
| Quotes / emphasis | Univers LT Std Light Oblique, Arial Italic | Arial Italic | as body | KPMG Blue |
| Legal / footer | Arial | Arial | 5–7 pt | KPMG Blue or Grey |

## Usage patterns
- Headlines are set in KPMG Bold, tight leading, often split across two lines in two colours (e.g. "KPMG Global" white + "tech report 2026" Pacific Blue `#00B8F5`).
- Body text is predominantly **KPMG Blue, not black**. 134k of ~180k measured characters were `#00338D`.
- White text is used on Cobalt/KPMG Blue full-bleed backgrounds; Pale Blue `#ACEAFF` and Pacific Blue `#00B8F5` are used for highlighted words and numbers on dark backgrounds.
- Large section numbers (Contents page, tech report) are KPMG Bold in white with a short label in Univers Light.

## Notes
- KPMG Bold and Univers are licensed fonts and are **not** included here. Use Arial for Word/PowerPoint per the KPMG branding skill.
- Minimum sizes from the brand skill: Arial no smaller than 9 pt in print or 14 px on screen (legal text excepted).
