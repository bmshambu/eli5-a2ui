# KPMG AI

Every page is a deep blue night lit by neon. The AI Pulse deck keeps its pages **blue, not black**: neon photography sits under a Cobalt wash, and all numbers glow in Pacific Blue. Use it for anything about AI.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page wash | Cobalt over neon photo | `#1E49E2` at 80–90% | the default page background |
| Deepest areas, vignettes | Ink Navy | `#050B2E` | corners and fades only |
| Numbers, rings, bars, rules | Pacific Blue | `#00B8F5` | the glow colour, add a soft outer glow |
| Second-line headline | Pacific Blue | `#00B8F5` | "AI Quarterly / **Pulse Survey**" |
| Hero stat bubble | Light Purple | `#B497FF` | one per page at most (Pulse p4 "83%") |
| Neon accents in photos | Purple / Magenta | `#7213EA` / `#AB0D82` | from imagery, not flat fills |
| Small text on blue | Pale Blue / White | `#ACEAFF` / `#FFFFFF` | |

Gradient text for one key phrase per page: Pacific Blue → Light Purple.

## Typography

- **Display:** KPMG Bold, White, with the second line or key phrase in Pacific Blue. 100–112 px cover, 44–52 px page titles.
- **Stats:** KPMG Bold 36–56 px in Pacific Blue, label 13–14 px White or Pale Blue to the right of the number (Pulse layout), not underneath.
- **Column heads:** a short 4 px Pacific Blue rule above each bold White head (Pulse "Executive Summary").
- **Body:** Arial / Univers Light 13–16 px White.

## Layout rules

1. **Cover:** full-bleed neon photo, Cobalt fade from the left, logo top-left, eyebrow in Pacific Blue, two-line title, date or strap bottom-left.
2. **Highlights page:** 3–4 column grid split by thin Pale Blue rules. Ring stats (Pacific Blue arc on 15% Pale Blue track), capsule progress bars (10 px tall, rounded, glowing fill), and numbered capsule pills ("01. Cybersecurity") in solid Pacific Blue with KPMG Blue text.
3. **Glass panels:** semi-transparent blue fill, 1 px Pacific Blue border with a soft outer glow. Use for groups, never for single lines.
4. **Footer:** small KPMG logo bottom-left, legal text in Pale Blue at 9–10 px.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the page blue. Pure black backgrounds belong to the photo, not to the layout.
- Do limit glow to numbers, rings and borders. Body text never glows.
- Don't use Magenta or Purple as flat fills. They come in through the photos.
- Don't put more than one Light Purple hero bubble on a page.
